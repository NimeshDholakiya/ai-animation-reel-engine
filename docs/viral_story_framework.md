# Viral Story Sourcing, Retention Curve & Connectors

## 1. Where to Source High-Engagement Stories
Never guess what people want to watch. Use pre-validated story goldmines:

### Sourcing Channels
- **Reddit (`r/AskReddit`, `r/Confessions`):**
  Search top monthly threads: *"What is the strangest / creepiest secret you ever witnessed?"*
- **Reddit (`r/NoSleep` & Creepypasta):**
  Short, suspenseful 30-second mysteries with sudden unexpected twists.
- **The "Inversion" Comedy Formula (e.g., *Baby Mafia*):**
  Take a terrifying mobster, ancient samurai, or elite bodyguard and put them in a harmless, wholesome, or silly everyday situation (e.g., babysitting, kindergarten dress code, feeding a kitten).

## 2. The Universal 30-Second Retention Timeline

```
0:00 ─── 0:03  [THE PATTERN INTERRUPT]  A bizarre visual or sound that stops scrolling.
0:03 ─── 0:08  [THE PREMISE & STAKES]   Who is here, and what do they want?
       ↳ CONNECTOR 1: "Lekin ek problem thi..."
0:08 ─── 0:18  [THE ESCALATION]         The character tries to fix it, but makes it worse.
       ↳ CONNECTOR 2: "Aur phir wahi hua jiska darr tha..."
0:18 ─── 0:26  [THE TWIST / CLIMAX]     Unexpected punchline, reveal, or sudden disaster.
0:26 ─── 0:30  [THE LOOP HOOK]          A freeze frame or funny reaction that loops seamlessly to 0:00!
```

## 3. Plug-and-Play Connectors Swipe File

### Narrative Connectors (English & Hindi)
* **The Escalation Connector (Between Seconds 5–10):**
  - *ENG:* *"Everything was going fine... until they noticed one tiny detail."*
  - *HIN:* *"सब कुछ ठीक चल रहा था... जब तक उसने वो एक छोटी सी चीज़ नहीं देखी।"*
* **The "Mistake" Connector (Between Seconds 10–18):**
  - *ENG:* *"And that was the exact moment he realized... he messed up."*
  - *HIN:* *"और ठीक उसी सेकंड उसे समझ आया... कि उससे बहुत बड़ी गलती हो चुकी है।"*
* **The Twist Connector (Before the Climax):**
  - *ENG:* *"Nobody expected what was waiting on the other side."*
  - *HIN:* *"लेकिन दरवाज़े के पीछे जो था... उसकी उम्मीद किसी ने नहीं की थी।"*

### Audio & Sound Connectors
1. **J-Cut:** Next scene's audio starts 0.5s before the video cut.
2. **Impact Whoosh:** Fast air-whoosh directly on every cut.
3. **The Dead Silence Drop:** Cut all background music dead 0.5s before the comedic punchline.
